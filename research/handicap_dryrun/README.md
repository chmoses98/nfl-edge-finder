## Known limitations visible in this run

1. **No current-season football exists.** No 2026 snap has been played, so every team and quarterback profile
   is labelled `prior_season_2025_no_games_played_in_2026`. This is the single biggest weakness of a Week-1
   packet and it is stated, not hidden.
2. **1,781 player markets have unresolved Kalshi→GSIS identity** and carry no model view. They are shown so
   the handicapper can still reason about them qualitatively.
3. **Roughly half of supported markets are untradable** — in NE@SEA, 161 of 349 were excluded from the
   disagreement ranking as untraded 0.00/0.99 books or books wider than 15 cents.
4. **Market movement is nearly empty.** Captures began 2026-09-04 and kickoff is 2026-09-09, so almost every
   horizon reports `horizon not yet reached`. This fills in on its own as the week progresses.
5. **No offensive-line quality metric.** We capture no blocking grades and none is invented; only OL injury
   status is reported.
6. **The model disagrees violently with the market on some player props** (e.g. P(McCaffrey 5+ receptions)
   0.16 model vs 0.51 market). Given the model is known to be redundant to the market on props, large
   disagreements on thin Week-1 role assumptions should be treated as a model warning, not an opportunity.
