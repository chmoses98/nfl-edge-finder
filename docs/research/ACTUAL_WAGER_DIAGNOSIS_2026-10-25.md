# Diagnosis of the owner's 2026 actual wagers (weeks 1-7), as of 2026-10-25

**Redacted: ratios, counts and z-scores only.** The repository is public; dollar amounts, tickers and per-position rows are in the private
form (`WAGER_DIAGNOSIS.md`, produced locally by `scripts/handicap/wager_diagnostics.py --out-private`). Method and code:
`nfl_edge/handicap/wager_diagnostics.py`. Regenerated by `actual-wagers.yml` into the redacted file `data/handicap/diagnostics/2026.wager_diagnostics.redacted.json`
on `market-data`.

Provenance first: **all 209 orders are owner fills routed by the Kalshi router.** The `handicap-data` ledger holds no real recommendation (three
TEST_ONLY records), so none of these wagers can be attributed to a model or to ChatGPT, and no thesis, probability or usage projection was recorded
with any of them. That limits what can be *proven* about cause (below), and it is the reason recommendation attribution now exists.

## Result

* 199 positions, **net ROI -20.2% of gross outlay**; fees alone 3.3% of outlay. Win rate 45.7% against 46.2% expected at the prices paid.
* Game-clustered bootstrap (114 independent clusters), ROI 5-95%: **-36.6% to -4.2%**.
* **Against a fair-price null** (every contract pays with probability equal to its price; held-to-settlement positions, 194 of them): gross profit before
  fees sits **-0.95 sd** from zero, net of fees **-1.26 sd**; 90 wins against 89.5 expected. The held positions did what their prices implied; fees took the rest.

| piece of the loss | share of the net loss | note |
|---|---|---|
| 194 positions held to settlement | about 60% | ROI -13.3%; of which fees are about a quarter |
| **5 full cashouts** | about 40% | ROI -89% on 9% of outlay; hindsight says holding would not have beaten the exits (the positions moved against the owner) |
| top 3 losing positions | 20% of gross losses | net would be roughly -44% of the actual figure without them |
| worst 3 games (parlays excluded: they have no game) | about 51% of the net loss | the worst carried 14U combined (a spread bought and then cashed out); the 16U game (KC at LV) was a net winner |

## Entry quality, fees, value

* **Entry CLV** (close mid of the side held minus the price paid): canonical **-0.57c per contract** (n=91, 48 games, 90% interval -0.74c to -0.42c);
  capture fallback **-0.72c** (n=49, 22 games). Only 9% (canonical) and 16% (fallback) of entries beat the close mid; 13% and 18% were more than a cent
  above it. That is a buyer who crosses the spread at a market that does not move afterwards: **bought at the number, not above it.**
* **Slippage:** not measurable. No depth is recorded at the moment of any fill (historical books exist only from 2026-09-04 and are not joined to fills).
* **Fees:** 3.3% of outlay overall; 4.2% on parlays; 5-6% on contracts under 30c (the fee is a larger share of a cheap contract).
* **Winners that were poor value:** of 46 winners with a valid CLV, 37 were bought above the close mid and 5 more than a cent above it; 3 losers were
  bought below the close. A winning bet bought above the close is a result, not evidence of skill.
* **Buys versus cashouts:** held 194 (ROI -13.3%) vs cashouts 5 (-89%); cashouts in hindsight were worth slightly *more* than holding.
* **Correlated exposure:** 44 of 77 games carried two or more positions (spread plus moneyline plus totals are one game state); 2 games exceeded the
  8U per-game cap on combined outlay (14U and 16U); weekly gross outlay ranged 12U to 54U (recycled capital included).
* **Stake escalation after a loss:** 45 flags (a stake more than 1.5x the previous realised loser) and 25 correlated-exposure flags; reporting only.

## Verdicts on the ten candidate causes

| # | cause | verdict | evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Poor football predictions | **NOT_MEASURABLE** | No recorded probability or thesis. The closest test (results vs the fair-price null) is -0.95 sd: consistent with prices; it cannot show predictions were fine |
| H2 | Correct theses through inferior markets | **NOT_MEASURABLE** | Theses were not recorded. Descriptive family spread only (above) |
| H3 | Overpaying | **SUPPORTED as cost only** | Fees 3.3% plus about half a spread (-0.6c); not paying above the close |
| H4 | Unrealistic player-usage assumptions | **NOT_DISTINGUISHABLE** | 58 prop positions at -19%, but no usage projection recorded; per-stat samples 1-16 |
| H5 | Overweighting unvalidated model probabilities | **NOT_MEASURABLE** | No fill is linked to a model number or a recommendation |
| H6 | Excessive correlation | **CONSISTENT** | about 51% of the net loss sits in 3 games; 44 of 77 games carry several positions; 2 games above 8U |
| H7 | Entering after value disappeared | **NOT_SUPPORTED** | Mean CLV -0.6c to -0.7c, no late-entry deterioration visible; 13-18% of entries a cent above the close |
| H8 | Sizing errors | **SUPPORTED** | 3 positions above the owner's own 5U maximum; the typical position is 0.75U, so a handful of oversized positions decided the season. Capping every stake at 5U (linear) would have cut the net loss by about 14% |
| H9 | Unfavourable or assumed liquidity | **NOT_MEASURABLE** | No depth at fill; fills of the 13U positions cannot be checked against displayed size |
| H10 | Ordinary variance | **CONSISTENT** | Net result is 1.26 sd below "fair prices minus fees"; 114 clusters; a loss of this size is what fair-priced betting plus fees plus a few oversized positions produces |

**Reading it plainly.** The sample cannot say the football predictions were poor, and cannot say they were good. What it does show is mechanical:
(1) the typical position was small and fairly priced, and fees (3.3%) plus crossing the spread are a certain drag; (2) a few very large positions (13U, 13U,
6U) and five cashouts carried most of the realised loss; (3) parlays and sub-30c longshots carry the heaviest fee load and lost the most per dollar;
(4) entries were at the market's number, with no sign of chasing. None of this is evidence of edge. It says where money can be saved without
having an opinion: stake caps, one budget per game, no parlays, no longshot tickets, and fewer, larger-edge bets.

## What changed in response

Owner policy `owner-2026-10-v1` (5U cap enforced, combined game exposure, correlation group budget), the stake ladder (larger stakes need corroborated
evidence), a card that PASSes unless a net edge of 2c survives fees at the conservative end, recommendation attribution, and this diagnosis rebuilt automatically.
Parlays are not derivable and are not recommended by the card builder.
