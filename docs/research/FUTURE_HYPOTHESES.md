# Future hypotheses (not tested, not tracked)

These ideas came up while implementing Wave 2. **None of them is a Wave-2 candidate.** Each would need its own
pre-registration before any data is joined.

* **WF-TOTAL sign.** The frozen 2026 WF-TOTAL fold puts a *negative* weight on `gap.total` (−0.20 per point of
  football baseline above the market). The procedure therefore leans against the football model's total
  disagreement, and its prospective test is of the procedure as frozen. Whether that sign is stable across folds
  is a separate, retrospective question.
* **Inactive-player settlement in prop structure studies.** Kalshi settles an inactive player's receptions market
  NO. Wave 1 was conditional on playing; Wave 2 records `played` on every RB-receptions row, so the two populations
  can be told apart. A dedicated study of pregame inactivity risk priced into NO asks is a different hypothesis.
* **Ladder coherence.** Non-monotone adjacent rungs occur in both sports. A descriptive coherence index could
  inform a future market-center rule.
